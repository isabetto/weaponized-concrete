# Surface brief: portfolio (all routes)

Mode: Experience (the work leads). Audience: Dutch recruiters, design leads. Job: decide in under a minute to open a case and invite her. Action: open a case, then mail/call. Proof: three projects (screens, demo videos), two theses with Dutch summaries. Constraints: Dutch only; WCAG 2.2 AA; React + Vite + Tailwind v4 on Vercel; no invented results; case content beyond the existing texts is clearly marked placeholder. Palette is free (user allowed a new one); her yellow survives only as a highlighter.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a printed research poster, because Isa's claim is design backed by research. It refuses the template of coloured section bands and equal project cards.
OWN-WORLD: Cool white paper, near-black navy ink, one committed cobalt field, highlighter yellow used only as a marker on key words. Bricolage Grotesque for headlines and UI, Source Serif 4 for reading text. Hairline column rules, crop marks on figure plates, "Figuur n." captions, definition lists for metadata. Placeholder plates are hatched with a dashed frame.
STORY: A hiring reader sees a designer who thinks in method, opens one case that reads Context, Aanpak, Resultaat like a short paper, believes the research claim, and mails her.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width cobalt title field: headline in white at about 88px across 8 columns, "onderzoekende blik." marked in highlighter; right 4 columns a serif abstract and an author block (name, role, Den Bosch, MSc New Media Design). Directly under the fold edge, Figuur 1 starts, so the work is visible at once. No hero buttons.
FORM: Research poster, position 7 of 7 grounded candidates (assigned by the roll), with the Riso challenger's two-spot-ink discipline raised into the palette rule (few inks, total commitment). seed:ab1d2218345a59f0
Signature interaction: the highlighter sweep (one authored moment) plus figure plates that lift on hover.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
